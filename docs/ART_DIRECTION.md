# Art direction and provenance

## Target look

Original, high-contrast pixel-art arcade platformer with cinematic, readable character silhouettes. Cutscenes should evoke the theatrical framing and rhythmic pacing of classic TMNT-era pixel arcade story panels without copying those games' characters, sprite sheets, compositions, layouts, logos, or exact art.

- Internal canvas: 320 × 180; scaled to 16:9 with image-rendering pixelated/crisp-edges.
- Shape language: clear 1–3 pixel highlights, dark navy outlines, chunky equipment, bright face/eye pixels, expressive body posture.
- Story panels: short captions, letterbox bars, visual focal point, brief holds, limited palette, quick fade/cut transitions.
- City: navy/steel skyline, broken cables, muted amber windows, red warnings.
- Forest: layered trunks and canopies, teal shadow, green fireflies, warm trail lights.
- Factory: heavy steel geometry, conveyor belts, industrial teal/grey, furnace orange and warning red.
- Every enemy threat must have a readable silhouette and a clear gameplay telegraph.

## Current original assets

All PNG sprites under `public/assets/sprites/` are generated with simple shapes by `scripts/generate_sprites.py` for this project. They are original prototype assets and can be regenerated. Background scenery and cutscene props are drawn from code in `public/js/art.js`.

Sprites: `human_amber.png`, `human_cyan.png`, `human_rose.png`, `robot_sentinel.png`, `robot_flyer.png`, `robot_creeper.png`, `giant_pursuer.png`, `crate.png`, `checkpoint_shelter.png`.

The original base PNGs remain for title staging and machines. Gameplay survivors now use a cached sixteen-pose animation sheet generated from original pixel code, with four palettes and four frame slots per pose. Native-scale silhouettes, frame boundaries and browser rendering are checked in the QA harness.


## October 2026 campaign presentation

The owner authorized 2D anime comic presentation for story scenes. Original story-atlas.png contains four regional illustrations, generated with the built-in imagegen tool; exact prompt and provenance are in STORY_ART_PROMPT.md. Native-text caption cards reveal with three focused, aspect-preserving crops per story beat. Gameplay remains crisp pixel art. Reduced-motion media preferences suppress panel motion. No external reference images were used.

Sound cues are original Web Audio oscillator synthesis in public/js/audio.js, with no sampled recordings. Saved sound/volume controls and gesture unlocking are implemented; physical speaker/headphone review remains pending.

## Original procedural motion, October 30

Ordinary gameplay robots now use a cached original 1152x192 sheet generated by robot-art.js. Four silhouettes have patrol, warning, attack, grab, stunned and recovery frames. Open hooked arms identify grabbers; raised shields anticipate a sentinel strike; flying twin nacelles surround a hanging scanner lens; bomber tracks and lifting vent plates indicate the blast. Semantic pose changes remain active with reduced motion while decorative frame cycling freezes. Ground danger markers, text countdowns and scan outlines remain separate authoritative-state cues.

Reduced motion also freezes survivor breathing/run-frame cycling and giant gait, and suppresses loose salvage particles. Position, collision and warning/capture timers continue normally; semantic jump/carry/capture/warning poses remain visible. A renderer comparison checks equal pixels at different decorative times under reduced motion and different pixels when an actual threat state changes.

Gameplay survivors use original pixel frames in art.js: idle breathing, four-phase run, jump/fall, stationary/running/airborne carry and throw, ladder climb, captured struggle, hit/kneel and down. The generated sheet is cached once and reused. Four coat palettes and numbered online labels distinguish participants. The original PNGs remain used for title/story staging. Robot patrol legs, grab arms, recovery sensors, scanner columns and named attack countdowns are drawn from original code. Hit/throw timers are authoritative visual metadata and do not change collision or heart rules. No external sprite sheet is used. The companion story-details.png atlas adds distinct rescue, covered-walkway, assembly and ambush artwork; see STORY_DETAILS_PROMPT.md. Water ripples and conveyor treads respect reduced motion, with fixed direction arrows retained. The Level 15 Colossus has warning/attack/stunned poses and a visible central core terminal with three progress lights.
