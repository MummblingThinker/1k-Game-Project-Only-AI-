# Campaign outline

## Arc I — Escape the City (Levels 1–5)

1. **Broken Boulevard** — first robot sweep; establish running, jumping, crates, and cover.
2. **Subway Shadows** — service platforms, search beams, and dead transit machinery.
3. **Rooftop Run** — fire escapes, stacked climbs, moving ledges, and high drops.
4. **The Siren District** — lure a patrol away from a gate and find a quiet route.
5. **City Gate** — last perimeter push. After victory, a cinematic shows robots flooding through the gate and chasing civilians into the forest.

Opening cinematic: humans try to flee as the robot invasion begins; survivors realize they must work together.

## Arc II — Through the Forest (Levels 6–10)

6. **Mosslight Trail** — deep forest and luminous route markers.
7. **River of Sparks** — river gorge, logs, exposed power cables, bridge-building.
8. **Watcher Canopy** — flying patrols search through branches.
9. **The Old Ranger Station** — abandoned human outpost and hidden route.
10. **The Covered Way** — the party reaches a covered human-only walkway.

Level 10 ending sequence: roughly one hundred flying robots crowd and swarm the sky above the covered way, but cannot pass through its human-only entrance. The survivors reach the sealed facility. The camera slowly pans through a robot-making line: robot frames, arms and heads are assembled on conveyors while pursuers enter behind the survivors. End the level on this unsettling reveal.

## Arc III — The Robot Factory (Levels 11–15)

11. **Assembly Line A** — first production floor and robot assembly belts.
12. **Furnace Hall** — timed machinery and furnace hazards.
13. **Prototype Wing** — experimental machines with unpredictable but telegraphed patterns.
14. **The Control Foundry** — reroute power and reach the production controls.
15. **Heart of Production** — destroy the factory's central production heart.

Level 15 ending: the human survivors celebrate, briefly believing they have ended the robot threat. Red sensor lights awaken behind them. The robots attack and kill the celebrating humans; cut sharply to black. This is the default story ending.

## Authoring requirement

This list is the narrative structure, not a completed map list. Build one authored geometry/encounter script per level, add unique obstacles and environmental storytelling, and ensure every required route remains solvable if only one player survives. Normal-length maps get one checkpoint around the middle; large maps get two around one-third and two-thirds.
